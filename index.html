Check a medicine’s scratch code — free SMS

For packs enrolled in NAFDAC’s Mobile Authentication Service (MAS), scratch the panel and send its PIN to the approved shortcode printed on the pack. NAFDAC describes this SMS check as toll-free in Nigeria.

You review and send the SMS. The provider’s reply arrives in Messages. The PIN is not saved or sent to this website’s server.

Use the shortcode supplied with your medicine. This list comes from NAFDAC’s published MAS guidelines; if yours is different, confirm it with NAFDAC. Some goods have no MAS panel. No response means the check is unresolved.
